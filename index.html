Our Story

A Cafe Unlike Any Other

Tucked behind a yellow egg sign in Jubilee Hills, The Hole in the Wall Café is Hyderabad's most eclectic multi-level dining experience. From the open-sky courtyard with pink umbrellas to the red brick art walls and hand-painted European murals — every corner tells a story. Come for the craft coffee, stay for the vibes.
